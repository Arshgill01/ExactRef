# Product

## Register
product

## Users
An operator (or an agent acting for one) who just received a CALL-E structured result containing an exact identifier: confirmation number, ticket, off-hire reference, order id. They are about to write it into a record. They are not browsing analytics.

## Product Purpose
ExactRef decides whether a spoken identifier may be written as a fact. Schema-valid plus a conversational “yes” is not enough. The product shows intended vs extracted at character level, names the provenance, and refuses silent substitution.

## Brand Personality
Plain, exact, slightly severe. Like a clerk’s stamp, not a startup dashboard.

## Anti-references
Generic AI ops consoles, metric-card grids, OffHire’s multi-model theater, dark “control room” cosplay, Inter-on-slate SaaS, hero blocks inside tools, pill overload.

## Design Principles
- The characters are the interface. If F became S, that is the only thing that matters on the page.
- Provenance is a named state, never a green check.
- Fixture replay is the default path; live calling is opt-in and fail-closed.
- One page, one identifier field, one disposition. An exception may hold several pages.
- Off-hire is the proof exception (`OH-01`), not a second product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. Status is text plus shape, not color alone. Focus visible. Escape closes dialogs. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables motion. No claim of certified WCAG conformance.
